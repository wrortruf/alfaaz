The Story

It started with a glimpse.

Barish ki halki boondein, college ke aas-paas ki khamoshi, ek café aur ek anjaan si ladki.

Ek simple si pehli jhalak ne ek overthinker ke dimaag mein poori duniya create kar di. Ek smile ek signal ban gayi. Ek “Hi” ek chapter ban gaya. Ek chhota sa gesture ek universe.

Story aage badhti hai — pehli baat-cheet se bonding tak, bonding se confession tak, aur phir un sawaalon tak jinke answers shayad kabhi mile hi nahi.

Dheere-dheere woh kahani sirf pyaar ki nahi rehti. Yeh trust, fear, silence, overthinking aur letting go ki kahani ban jaati hai.

Aur ant mein ek painful realization:

Kabhi-kabhi pyaar haar jaata hai— humse nahi, balki humare hi darron se.

Before You Read

Yeh sirf ek kahani nahi hai.

Har dard ke peeche ek muskaan chhupi thi, har aansoon ke peeche ek kahani khadi thi.

Jahan sabne samjha bas ek overthinking ka jaadoo hai, wahan yahi jaadoo mera sabse bada sach ban gaya.

Yeh kitab un palon ka bojh hai jo dil ne khud uthaya. Jahan har khushi chhoti si thi, aur har gham ek samundar jaisa.

Agar yeh kahani aapke dil ko chhoo jaaye, toh samajh lena — yeh sirf meri nahi, shayad hum sabki kahani ka ek hissa hai.

Some stories aren't written to be famous— they're written to be felt....
— Lafzon ka safar

About The Author

Pranjal Paul

I am Pranjal Paul, a writer who has always been fascinated by the magic of words — their ability to capture emotions, thoughts and moments that often remain unspoken.

Writing, especially shayari, poetry and stories, has always been my way of exploring feelings and turning them into something that another person can understand.

I find inspiration in ordinary moments — a fleeting glance, a shared smile, a rainy evening, or the quiet reflection of the mind at night.

Through my words, I want readers to pause, reflect and perhaps find fragments of their own stories hidden somewhere between the lines.

Every piece I create is a window into my mind and soul.

A Message for Future Me

Some words are not meant for today.

LAFZON KA SAFAR SOMEWHERE IN THE FUTURE

Dear Future Me,

Agar tum ye padh rahe ho, toh shayad bahut kuch badal chuka hoga.

Shayad tum ab woh insaan nahi rahe jo ye lafz likh raha hai. Shayad tumhare paas naye log hain, naye sapne hain, naye sheher hain, aur zindagi ki kuch aisi kahaniyan hain jinke baare mein aaj ka main soch bhi nahi sakta.

Lekin ek baar rukna...

Aur mujhe yaad karna.

Us ladke ko, jo har chhoti si baat ko dil mein rakh leta tha. Jo ek smile mein poori kahani dekh leta tha. Jo kisi ke ek “theek ho?” ke peeche bhi hazaar meanings dhoondhne lagta tha.

Us ladke ko yaad karna jo bahut kuch kehna chahta tha, lekin aksar keh nahi paata tha.

Jiske paas lafz bahut the, par jab dil ki baat kehne ka waqt aaya, toh wahi lafz kahin kho gaye.

✦

Mujhe nahi pata tumne apni zindagi mein kitna kuch paa liya hoga.

Shayad woh sapne poore ho gaye honge jinke baare mein main aaj raaton ko sochta hoon. Shayad tumne woh kitaab likh di hogi jise main aaj sirf imagine karta hoon. Shayad tumhare naam ke saath woh sab kuch jud gaya hoga jiska sapna humne chupke se dekha tha.

Lekin agar nahi hua...

Toh please, khud ko failure mat kehna.

Tumne koshish ki thi. Aur kabhi-kabhi zindagi mein itna hi kaafi hota hai.

Mujhe bas ye yaad rakhna hai ki humne har baar perfect hone ki koshish nahi ki thi... humne bas sachcha rehne ki koshish ki thi.

Humne likha, jab bol nahi paaye.

Humne muskuraya, jab andar se sab theek nahi tha.

Humne logon ko khone ke baad bhi unke liye bura nahi socha.

Aur sabse zaroori...

Humne pyaar karna nahi chhoda.

♡

Future Me, agar kabhi tumhe lage ki tum bahut aage aa gaye ho, toh peeche mud kar ek baar is jagah ko dekh lena.

Ye website... ye kitaab... ye lafz... ye saari adhuri baatein...

Ye sab us version of you ki nishani hai jo shayad duniya ke liye bahut simple tha, lekin apne andar ek poori duniya lekar chalta tha.

Aur agar kabhi tumhe lage ki tumhare andar ka woh writer kahin kho gaya hai, toh please usse dhoond lena.

Kyunki usne tumhe sirf kahaniyan nahi di thi...

usne tumhe khud ko samajhne ka ek tareeka diya tha.

Aur agar kabhi tum kisi ko bahut pyaar karo, toh iss baar chup mat rehna.

Jo kehna ho, keh dena.

Jo mehsoos karna ho, mehsoos kar lena.

Kyunki shayad zindagi ka sabse bada dard kisi ko kho dena nahi hota...

Shayad sabse bada dard ye hota hai ki humne usse kabhi bataya hi nahi ki woh humare liye kitna zaroori tha.

Aur haan...

Agar aaj tum khush ho, toh meri taraf se tumhe ek tight hug.

Aur agar aaj bhi tum thode se tootey hue ho, toh koi baat nahi.

Tumhe har waqt strong rehne ki zaroorat nahi hai.

Bas rukna mat.

Thoda dheere chalna, lekin chalna.

Aur jab kabhi lage ki sab kuch khatam ho gaya... toh yaad rakhna, ek baar humne bhi socha tha ki kuch dard kabhi khatam nahi honge.

Lekin dekho... tum yahan tak aa hi gaye.

─────────

Bas ek promise karna...

Jo ladka tumhe yahan tak lekar aaya hai, usse kabhi sharminda mat karna.

Uske sapno ko zinda rakhna. Uske lafzon ko zinda rakhna. Aur sabse badhkar... uske dil ko zinda rakhna.

With all the feelings I couldn't explain today,

— Pranjal The version of you who still believes in words.

Kuch Log Yaadon Mein Reh Jaate Hain

Kuch log bas yaadon mein reh jaate hain.

Aaj khaamoshi ke saaye mein so jaate hain.
Pal do pal ki khaamoshi thi unke chehre par,
Aur hum the unki duniya ke ghere par.
Phir waqt ne aisa mod liya,
Ki har khushi ne bhi rona sikh liya.
Ab aksar raaton mein unki yaad aati hai,
Kahin se unki hansi goonj jaati hai.
Zindagi ke safar mein sab kuch milta hai,
Bas wo log nhi milta jo dil ko chhoo jaataa hai.

— Pranjal Paul

05 WHY I WRITE

Maybe writing is just another form of feeling.
